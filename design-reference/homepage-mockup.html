Underdogs Steal Overtime Thriller On Blocked Kick

A fourth-string kicker's nightmare becomes the story of the season, as the home side survives a two-point-conversion scare to escape with a 27–26 win.

By M. Alvarez  ·  Staff Correspondent  ·  Filed From Section 114

Nobody in the press box saw it coming. With eleven seconds left and the visiting side lined up for what should have been a routine game-winner, a walk-on special-teamer split the line untouched and got just enough fingertip on the ball to send it fluttering wide of the post.

What followed was a delirious sprint of bodies onto the turf, a delay of nearly six minutes, and a locker room celebration that the beat writers on hand described as somewhere between a pep rally and a fire drill. Overtime, when it finally arrived, felt almost anticlimactic by comparison.

Still, there was drama left to spare. The home offense went three-and-out on its first possession, and the defense was forced to make a fourth stop of the night on third-and-goal from the six.

Afterward, the kicker who'd made the block, a sophomore walking on to just his third varsity special-teams snap of the year, said he'd been coached to do exactly one thing on that particular formation: get skinny and get up. "I got up," he said. "That's the whole story."
